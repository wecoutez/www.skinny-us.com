Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Skinny-us is a curated guide to Seoul, available as a website and an iOS app. This page explains what the app and the site do with information. The short version: we collect as little as possible, and the app keeps almost everything on your own device.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising. There are no third-party advertising or analytics trackers in the iOS app.

What stays on your device

The iOS app stores the following locally, on your phone only. It is not sent to us and we cannot see it:

  • Picks you save
  • Your language preference (English or Korean)
  • Your access code, if you entered one
  • Cached photos, so the app loads faster and uses less data

Deleting the app removes all of it.

What the app requests from the internet

To show current picks and photos, the app downloads content files from skinny-us.com and photos from Google's Places service. These are ordinary web requests. As with any web request, the servers involved may log technical information such as an IP address and the time of the request. We do not use these logs to build profiles of individuals.

Purchases

Purchases in the iOS app are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Apple tells the app whether a purchase is currently active — nothing more. Apple's own privacy policy covers that transaction.

If you subscribed through the website instead, that payment was handled by PayPal under PayPal's privacy policy.

If you contact us

If you send a message through the website's chat or by email, we keep your name, email address and message so we can reply. We use it only to answer you. Ask us to delete it and we will.

Children

Skinny-us is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Clear saved picks and cached photos in the app under You.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
  • Delete the app to remove all local data.
  • Email us to ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top of this page.
