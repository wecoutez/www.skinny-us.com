Your first visit

Bring with you

  • Photo ID or Alien Registration Card
  • Current medications
  • Recent health check-up results
  • Records from other clinics

Registration

Walk-in rather than by appointment. Early morning and around 3pm on weekdays are usually the quietest.

Access

  • 2 minutes from Jeongja Station Exit 3
  • Fifth floor, lift available
  • Building car park (ask about validation)

Consultations are conducted primarily in Korean, with English available. If you would prefer to bring an interpreter, you are welcome to.
