Abdominal & thyroid ultrasound

Echocardiography and carotid Doppler are listed under cardiac tests above; this section covers the other areas. Ultrasound uses sound waves above the range of human hearing to image internal organs and vessels. There is no radiation and no pain.

Gastroscopy

Gastric cancer is the most common malignancy in Korean men and the second most common in women. Upper abdominal disease cannot be diagnosed from symptoms alone, which is why direct observation of the oesophagus, stomach and duodenum matters.

  1. Fast for 6–8 hours before the examination.
  2. If you take blood pressure medication, discuss it with the doctor and take it with a small sip of water on the day. Tell us in advance about glaucoma, heart disease, prostate conditions or pregnancy.
  3. 10–20 minutes beforehand you will swallow a de-gassing solution (about 10cc), then hold a topical anaesthetic in the mouth for about 10 minutes.
  4. The examination itself takes 3–5 minutes; 10–20 minutes if a biopsy or treatment is performed.
  5. The throat anaesthetic wears off in about an hour. Do not eat or drink until then.
  6. Mild bloating or throat discomfort afterwards settles within 1–2 days. Biopsy results take about a week.
Tell us immediately if

you develop severe throat pain, severe abdominal pain, vomiting of blood, rectal bleeding or black stools after the examination.

Recommended annually from age 40 regardless of symptoms, since gastric cancer in Korea is frequently found in people with no symptoms at all.
