Subscription Management

To manage, view, or cancel your Skinny Premium subscription:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad
  2. Tap your Apple ID at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Select Skinny Premium to manage or cancel

Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period. You will continue to have access until then.

Restoring Purchases

If you've previously subscribed and need to restore your purchase on a new device:

  1. Open Skinny-us
  2. Tap the subscription paywall
  3. Tap "Restore purchases"

Common Questions

The app isn't loading content.
Make sure you have an active internet connection. Try closing and reopening the app.

I was charged but premium features aren't unlocked.
Tap "Restore purchases" on the paywall, or contact us at erinaranwon@gmail.com with your Apple ID purchase receipt.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > Skinny Premium > Cancel.
